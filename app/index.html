Road to the Platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving — and cheats only unlock after the story.

Stage 1 · 60–80 hours

Play the story

Five chapters, 12 story trophies. Take optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals on the way — the 100% checklist counts progress from the start even though you cannot see it until after the credits.

Meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1. It is the one spot that disappears — see Positive Mental Attitude.

Stage 2 · about 100 hours

The 100% checklist

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The bar is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 50 of 66 weapons, 300 stunts.

Stage 3 · about 10 hours

Strangers & cleanup

Finish the 8 stranger mission strands and the side-character finals, then sweep the remaining activities, robberies and collectibles. Most trophies fall automatically once the checklist is done.

Before you start: there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. The only trophy that can be missed is Positive Mental Attitude (the Chapter 1 meditation). Everything else stays available in free-roam after the story.
