⚠ The one that can slip away

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first spot is offered in Chapter 1, at Jason’s house after Down to Earth, when Lucia invites him to the balcony in the morning. Take it before Chapter 1 ends — after that it is gone for the playthrough.

Source: PowerPyx trophy roadmap & KeenGamer platinum guide. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.

      Roadmap to the platinum — six stages, about 200 hours

      Built from the official descriptions. Play the story first, protect the one missable meditation, then work the 100% checklist — which is kinder than it sounds: PowerPyx puts it near 60% true completion (200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 50 of 66 weapons, 70 of 80 challenges).

      Missables — exactly one

      Of all 55 trophies, only Positive Mental Attitude can be missed, and only its first meditation spot. Everything else stays earnable in free-roam after the story, and there is no mission replay — so the journal flags the one moment that matters and lets the rest wait.

      Note: some guides mention stranger strands having story-progress windows. No second trophy is confirmed missable, so this journal flags one and says so plainly.
